## What Happened

### Friday was the supply print. The weekend was the customer.

Anthropic, the artificial-intelligence developer behind the Claude models, raised $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation over the weekend, overtaking OpenAI as the most valuable private company in the field. It shipped its Claude Opus 4.8 model and disclosed a revenue run-rate near $47 billion, up from $30 billion. On a quiet weekend this is the demand-side counterpart to Friday's Dell-led repricing of the artificial-intelligence-server stack: the spend that lifts the memory and cloud cohort the portfolio owns is now underwritten by a customer whose own revenue is compounding, not by a sell-side forecast. Cloudflare (NET), a portfolio holding, runs Anthropic's agent sandboxes. Friday gave the trade a supply print; the weekend gave it a paying customer.

### Markets priced out a June cut.

Prediction markets moved over the weekend to price a near-certain hold at the Federal Reserve's June 16–17 rate meeting — Polymarket and Kalshi put June at roughly 97 percent no-change, and at about two-in-three odds of no cut at all in 2026. This is market-implied sentiment, not a Fed action, and it reconciles with Friday's in-line inflation reading as timing rather than regime: realized inflation from the February–April oil spike is still washing through — pump gasoline is up about 50 percent since late February — even as May's near-19-percent oil crash sets up the forward disinflation the prior brief flagged. The split is a lag, not a reversal. The May consumer-price report on June 11 is the settlement.

### Moderna's regulatory path cleared on two fronts.

Moderna (MRNA), a portfolio holding, drew two regulatory tailwinds over the weekend. An FDA advisory committee voted almost unanimously on May 28 to back an updated COVID vaccine targeting the XFG variant for the 2026–27 season, clearing the fall product path. Separately, the agency reversed course to review Moderna's messenger-RNA flu vaccine, with a decision expected around August 5. After a year in which regulatory headwind carried much of the bear case, both weekend items cut the other way — the COVID strain selection a modest near-term clearance, the flu review the larger binary catalyst. August 5 is the date that decides the bigger question.
