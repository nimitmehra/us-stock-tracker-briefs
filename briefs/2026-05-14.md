## What Happened

### Beijing Day 1. Hormuz stays open. Tariffs unresolved.

Donald Trump and Xi Jinping opened a Beijing summit on Wednesday — the first U.S. presidential visit to China in roughly a decade. The White House readout said both sides agreed the Strait of Hormuz must remain open, with Xi expressing interest in buying more American oil to reduce the chokepoint exposure. Xi called Taiwan the "most important issue", warning that mishandling could move the relationship to a "dangerous place". Jensen Huang, the Nvidia (NVDA) chief executive, joined Air Force One at the last minute after Trump called him in Alaska — the market read the optics as memory and accelerator export-curb easing back on the table. WTI sat at $101.01, Brent $105.92; the risk premium has settled into the price. Concrete tariff and export-control deliverables have not landed.

### Micron at $803. The trim is sixty percent in the money.

Micron Technology (MU), the largest American memory-chip maker, closed Tuesday at $803.63, up 4.83 percent and at a fresh all-time high — the seventh straight session above $700 and the ninth above the $500 trim line. Bank of America raised its price target to $950 from $500, almost doubling it, on an upgraded $1.7 trillion AI data-center addressable market. TrendForce reported DRAM contract prices up 58 to 63 percent quarter-on-quarter; Gartner forecast full-year DRAM +125 percent. The active $500 trigger is +60.7 percent in the money; the $700 sub-threshold +14.8 percent. Bank of America almost doubled the target. The desk does not need to.

### Bloom approaches $300. Day four.

Bloom Energy (BE), the solid-oxide fuel-cell maker, closed at $289.76, up 3.23 percent — 0.4 percent below its fresh 52-week high and roughly 3.5 percent below the $300 line. Oracle named Bloom the entire power source for its Project Jupiter AI data-center campus, replacing gas turbines and diesel generators at up to 2.8 gigawatts under an expanded agreement. The Day-4 structural re-rating arrives with a Motley Fool "once-in-a-decade" piece and a Cramer "rocket ship" call inside the same week — sentiment compounding the catalyst. The stock has gained 1,414 percent over the past year. A $300 close puts active trim discipline on the desk; the node carries no formal trigger to anchor it yet.
