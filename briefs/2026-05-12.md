### Oil bid; consumer cracked.

Underneath, the consumer cracked: the retail ETF (XRT) fell 3.61 percent, the worst sector on the tape; discount, off-price, mass-merchant, cruise and airline names were all crushed in a single session. April consumer-sentiment at 47.6, the lowest in the survey's 74-year history, is now pricing through every consumer cohort at once. Oil's gain is the retail tape's bill.
