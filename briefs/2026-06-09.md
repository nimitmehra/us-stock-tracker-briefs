## What Happened

### Taiwan Semiconductor told the bulls the demand is durable

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing (TSM), the contract chipmaker that builds the leading-edge silicon for NVIDIA and Apple, raised its 2026 revenue-growth guide above 30 percent overnight — the cleanest confirmed read on AI-capital-spending durability since Broadcom's softer artificial-intelligence guide erased roughly $923 billion of chip-sector value in early June. The guide matters more to the book than Monday's bounce did. It sits on the demand side of the same debate that drives the memory holding Micron Technology (MU) and the chip leader NVIDIA (NVDA). One foundry leader lifting full-year guide into a demand scare is the counterweight a long-only desk re-models the whole semiconductor curve on. Wednesday's inflation print, not the guide, decides whether the tape gets to act on it.
