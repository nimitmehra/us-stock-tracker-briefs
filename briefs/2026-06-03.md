## What Happened

Gox fears, and 3.4 billion dollars of exchange-traded fund outflows since May. The buildout trade had been priced as if the long end did not exist; today the long end answered.

The catalyst-supported breakout suggests the $560 add level matters sooner than the starter.

### Microsoft's regulatory week landed alongside an insider sell.

Microsoft (MSFT) fell 2.18 percent to $431.70 — its second straight loss as the regulatory and capex-overhang anchor of mega-cap tech. President Trump signed an executive order requiring artificial-intelligence companies to submit new models for federal review. The Federal Trade Commission was reported to be investigating Microsoft's cloud, AI and software bundling.
